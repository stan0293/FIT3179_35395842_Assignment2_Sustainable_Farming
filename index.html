Australia’s Farming Landscape

Farming occupies an uneven share of the landscape

South West Queensland has the highest agricultural land share among the 57 mapped regions, at 92.2%. Southern Gulf follows at 90.3%, while Western, New South Wales, records 90.0%.

Agriculture includes cropping, horticulture and livestock production, including grazing. A higher agricultural share does not necessarily mean more sustainable farming.
